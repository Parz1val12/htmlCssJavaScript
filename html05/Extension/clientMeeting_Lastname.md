## Research

- [What to Ask Clients Before Starting a Project - Wix.com](https://www.wix.com/studio/blog/questions-to-ask-a-client-before-taking-on-a-project)
   This page explained that designers should lead initial conversations by focusing on the client's high-level business goals and underlying challenges rather than just talking about website aesthetics.

- [Getting Started with Client Intake Forms - Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/resources/client-intake-form/)
   This page explained how a client intake form is used as a screening tool to collect essential business data and establish a clear onboarding process before scheduling a kickoff meeting.

- [Graphic design client questionnaire: 13 key questions - HoneyBook](https://www.honeybook.com/blog/graphic-design-client-questionnaire)
   This page explained that asking clients to share specific examples of designs they love and hate helps pinpoint their visual preferences and expectations.

## Questions

1. What is your business?

2. Who runs it?

3. Who is it intended for?

4. What does it do?

5. What pages do you want?

6. Who takes care of pics and videos for the website?

7. How much media do you want?

8. Will your media be updated frequently?

9. Do you already have a logo and/or colors?

10. Do we need to create a logo and/or colors?

11. What do you want the style to be?

12. Give me some websites you maybe like or dislike so I know what style you're looking for.

13. When do you want this done?

14. What is your budget?

15. Will you want updates to it?

16. Where will it be published?
