Kraft Mac-and-Cheese Recipe

This Web Page walkes you through how to make mac-and-cheese with the store bought Kraft box and at home ingredients

Disclaimer: You need to have the official Kraft Mac-and-Cheese box.

Home About Purchase

ingredients

Milk
1/4 cup
Butter
4 tbsp
Water
6 cups
Kraft Box
This includes the noodles and cheese packet.

Steps

  1. Pour 6 cups of water into a pot.
  2. Heat water until boiling.
  3. Add noodles and cook for 7:30 at a medium temperature with occasional stiring.
  4. Pour noodles into a strainer to remove all water.
  5. Pour water noodles back into pot.
  6. Add milk, butter, and cheese packet
  7. Stir till no clumps of butter or cheese are left, and noodles are equally creamy and cheesy
  8. Take the portion you want, put it in a bowl, grab a fork, and start eating.
